## 1. Measurable Acceptance Criteria

1. **Brand Positioning (`CLIENT-REV-01`, `02`):**
   - The landing page and dashboard clearly communicate **Youth Athletic Development & Strength & Conditioning** with the core message *"BUILD THE ATHLETE BEFORE CHASING PERFORMANCE."*

2. **Program Differentiation (`CLIENT-REV-15`, `16`):**
   - Parents can clearly distinguish between **Youth Athlete Performance** (for athletes with sports background) and **Multilateral Athletic Development** (for building fundamental movement literacy) including specific pricing structures.

3. **Assessment Reframing (`CLIENT-REV-05`, `06`):**
   - Assessment is presented as an internal decision-making tool for coaching. The form wizard supports an explicit **Beginner (Delta %)** vs. **Competitive (Benchmark)** mode toggle.

4. **Coaching Lifecycle (`CLIENT-REV-02`, `08`):**
   - Identified physical deficits from assessments guide the selection of corresponding exercise templates in the training plan builder.

5. **Parent Report Usability (`CLIENT-REV-10`, `12`):**
   - Parents can review assessment results via PDF and WhatsApp in under 30 seconds with plain Indonesian guidance and 6–8 week coach recommendations.

6. **Claims Compliance (`GLOBAL-19`):**
   - 100% of website and PDF report text is verified free of prohibited claims (zero "100% bebas cedera", zero medical diagnoses, zero ungrounded "standar internasional").

7. **Direct WhatsApp Booking (`CLIENT-REV-18`):**
   - All booking CTA buttons link directly to `https://wa.me/628886602440` (`+62 888-6602-440`).
